## Fill Workflow

1. Open `docs/ppt_fill.md` at the slide you are building.
2. Copy the bullets onto the slide as-is.
3. Draw the table shown there (if any) as a native PowerPoint table.
4. Insert the named image (if any) with the given caption.
5. Fill the blank underlines (names, rolls, guide, email) by hand.
