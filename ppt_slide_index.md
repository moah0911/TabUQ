## Three Rules Before You Touch a Slide

1. **One deck per review** — Copy the matching 10-slide review section (or the 20-slide Final deck) into your own file. Don't mix slides from different reviews.
2. **Slide titles are generalised — content is yours** — Follow the structure and thesis mapping shown on every slide. Replace bracketed placeholders and the "what to present" bullets with your project's actual content.
3. **Every slide maps to a thesis section** — The footer shows which thesis chapter/section it feeds. Finish the slide, then write that section within 1 week.

## Fill Workflow

1. Fill the **Global Placeholders** on the cover of whatever deck you are building first.
2. For each slide, complete the `WHAT TO PRESENT` bullets and replace `INSERT DIAGRAM / TABLE / SCREENSHOT HERE` with the actual visual.
3. The footer (`MAPS TO THESIS` / `SOURCED FROM`) tells you which thesis chapter the slide feeds — write that section within 1 week of finishing the slide.
4. Each review's slide 2 is the previous review's feedback-resolution slide — keep those BATCH remarks tracked.
