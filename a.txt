HOW TO USE THIS FRAMEWORK

Three Rules Before You Touch a Slide

01

One deck per review

Copy the matching 10-slide review section (or the 20-slide Final deck) into your own file. Don't mix slides from different reviews into one deck.

02

Slide titles are generalised — content is yours

Follow the structure and the thesis mapping shown on every slide. Replace bracketed placeholders and the "what to present" bullets with your project's actual content.

03

Every slide maps to a thesis section

The footer on each slide shows exactly which thesis chapter/section it feeds. Finish the slide, then write that section within 1 week — this is how the thesis builds itself.
